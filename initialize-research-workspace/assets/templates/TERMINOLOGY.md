## Maintenance

- For a new term, abbreviation, or proposed change, show the owner the source term, context, proposed wording, and supporting source. Update the register after explicit approval.
- Check meanings and manuscript usage against the designated current source or an appropriate subject reference. Record a locator, such as a section, definition, or bibliographic entry.
- While wording is under review, retain the source term or mark the proposed expression as pending in drafts. Check the current manuscript to establish whether it actually contains a method or result.
